# Accepted amendment to ADR 0001: Core v0.6 ingestion reconciliation

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-04
- **Amends:** [ADR 0001](../0001-policy-driven-standards-proficiency-and-grade-calculation.md)
- **Related decision:** [ADR 0003](../0003-consumer-side-producer-adapters.md)
- **Related architecture:** [Core v0.6 publication ingestion](../../architecture/core-v0.6-publication-ingestion.md)

## Purpose

ADR 0001 correctly assigns policy-driven proficiency and Grade calculation to
Meridian. This amendment reconciles its producer-neutrality and reproducibility
language with Core v0.6's released publication registry and Meridian's
consumer-side adapter architecture.

It does not alter the decision that producer evidence requires explicit
Meridian policy before becoming standards evidence or a Grade.

## Reconciliation

### Explicit producer integration

"Producer-specific evidence must enter Meridian through an explicit integration
contract" means:

1. Core's canonical Publication Record and referenced registration are reloaded;
2. withdrawal and supersession state are reloaded;
3. producer compatibility is evaluated through the metadata-only Core profile;
4. an exact Meridian adapter is selected;
5. authorization is enforced;
6. Core verifies the exact manifest path and digest; and
7. the adapter invokes the producer-owned public reader and projects validated
   native values.

Meridian must not parse arbitrary files or duplicate producer validators.
Calling a producer-owned public reader through a Meridian adapter is consumer
orchestration, not transfer of producer schema ownership.

### Separate decisions

The following remain independent:

- canonical Publication Record validity;
- producer profile compatibility;
- Meridian adapter support;
- producer manifest validity;
- authorization;
- evidence eligibility;
- Grade-item membership;
- attempt selection; and
- Grade or proficiency calculation.

A positive result at one stage does not imply a positive result at a later stage.

### Exact reproducibility

A Meridian evidence projection or calculation provenance must be capable of
identifying, as applicable:

- canonical `publication_id`;
- complete work identity;
- publication kind and record-set identity/revision;
- exact manifest contract, path, digest algorithm, and digest;
- exact referenced Academic Work Registration revision;
- producer contract version;
- optional source-record contract;
- predecessor and observed withdrawal state;
- producer reader/contract identity;
- Meridian adapter and projection-contract identity;
- selected and excluded native evidence;
- exact Academic Period Calendar revision;
- policy versions;
- overrides; and
- calculation time.

A producer, work ID, catalog row, package version, or path named `latest` is not
sufficient provenance.

### Native meaning

Adapters preserve all attempts or observations represented by the producer
contract, native scales, non-score states, and provenance. Question alignment is
not automatically a standards rating. Native ratings are not automatically
Meridian proficiency levels. Missing or incomplete states are not automatically
zero.

## Consequence

ADR 0001 remains accepted. Where its earlier general producer-integration
language could be read ambiguously, this amendment and ADR 0003 govern the Core
v0.6 ingestion boundary.
