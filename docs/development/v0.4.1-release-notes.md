# Meridian v0.4.1 release notes

Meridian v0.4.1 repairs configured authorization bootstrap across protected
teacher workflows and adds a ninth district-neutral report: **Benchmark Analysis
& Instructional Response Planner**.

The planner reuses Meridian's configured proficiency framework and common
teacher-report serialization/rendering. It keeps roster, expected, tested,
valid, missing, unassessed, insufficient, invalid, blank, and ambiguous states
distinct; labels every denominator; preserves custom proficiency scales; and never
averages ordinal proficiency labels. Optional groups, including a group named
Bubble, are teacher-authored planning metadata and cannot change proficiency,
Grades, producer evidence, or Grade Item selection.

The print-friendly Markdown representation has four sections: Assessment Data,
Standards and Item Analysis, Instructional Response, and Results from Targeted
Instruction. JSON, CSV, and TSV remain deterministic. Confirmed plans use stable
identity and append-only immutable revisions so the original analysis remains
available when follow-up observations are added.

Follow-up results are descriptive. Meridian does not claim that an intervention
caused an observed change, and a local planner is not an official SIS/LMS record
or proof of submission or approval.
