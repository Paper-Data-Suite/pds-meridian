## Required acceptance boundary

- protected workflow authorization is workspace-bound, teacher-local, exact,
  and fail-closed throughout diagnostics, menu, CLI, and live execution;
- the ninth report preserves native assessment semantics, separates roster,
  tested, valid, missing, not-assessed, invalid, and insufficient states, and
  never averages ordinal proficiency levels;
- standards and item analysis exclude blank or ambiguous evidence;
- instructional responses remain teacher-authored, include extension and
  follow-up planning, and persist only after explicit confirmation;
- saved plans form immutable append-only, SHA-linked revision chains;
- all nine reports execute through normal guided selection and direct CLI;
- source movement and authorization changes fail closed at execution and save
  time; and
- release artifacts and all supported platform/Python matrices qualify from
  the exact merged source.
