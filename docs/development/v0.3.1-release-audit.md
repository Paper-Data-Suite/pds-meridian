## Compatibility boundary

The manifest contract remains exactly
`scoreform_academic_result_manifest_v1`. The installed regression uses the
released public reader with punctuation-bearing Standards Profile and Standard
identities. Meridian must preserve the exact strings, retain source manifest
bytes unchanged, retain all attempts, and leave eligibility unevaluated.

No part of this patch may infer proficiency, mastery, newest/highest/best or
official evidence, currentness, Grade policy, Grade calculation, ReportingSnapshot
authority, or external-system authority merely from Standards alignment or the
reader-version change.
